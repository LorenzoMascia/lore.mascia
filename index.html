hw.01 — transmitter · thesis system

IR Transmitter

Mounted on the ceiling, it floods a cone-shaped zone with a coded 940nm infrared beacon — the invisible boundary the whole system is built on.

designed by me — circuit and driver stage on perfboard, embedded firmware, and the 3D-printed enclosure.

hw.02 — receiver · thesis system

IR Receiver

Rides on the shopping cart. When it crosses the transmitter's beacon it timestamps the event and ships it over Wi-Fi — the server turns it into a push notification.

designed by me — sensing and power circuit, battery supply with hard switch, firmware and uplink, plus the hooked enclosure that clips onto the cart.
